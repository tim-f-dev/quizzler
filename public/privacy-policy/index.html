Language preference and browser storage

The site saves your selected language (en or de) in your browser's local storage under quizzler-language, including the default English selection on first use. This keeps the language consistent across pages and later visits. The preference is read by this website's script and is not sent to the operator as part of a separate tracking request. It remains until you clear the website's browser data or change the selection. The purpose is to provide your preferred interface language; the legal basis for any associated personal-data processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The current website code contains no analytics, advertising trackers, account registration or contact forms and does not set application cookies. Cloudflare may use cookies or similar technologies for security depending on the services enabled.

Contact by email

If you email me, your address, message and any details you provide are processed to respond to your enquiry. Please only send information needed for your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on the interest in answering enquiries; Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where an enquiry concerns a contract. Messages are deleted when the enquiry is resolved and the data is no longer needed, unless a legal retention obligation or the defence of legal claims requires further storage. Email service providers may process the correspondence as part of delivering and storing email.
